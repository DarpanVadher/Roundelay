Roundelay shares what your Windows PC or Android phone is playing with every other device on your Wi-Fi or hotspot, and keeps them all in sync. Scan a QR code to join.

Free and open source. No account, no cloud.

Join in the time it takes to scan a code

No pairing menus, no accounts, no router settings. Everything happens on your own network.

  1. Start sharing on the device that is playing. Roundelay shows a QR code.
  2. Scan it with Roundelay on another device. The host asks you to approve the newcomer.
  3. Listen together. Add as many devices as the network can carry. Move the latency slider if the Wi-Fi is busy.

Built so the beat lands together

Clocks agree first
Each guest measures how far its clock is from the host's, using the quickest round trip it sees, then schedules every sound against the host's timeline.
Drift is corrected quietly
Crystals run at slightly different speeds. Roundelay nudges playback by under half a percent to stay aligned, with no clicks or gaps.
Latency you can tune
The default delay is 160 ms, chosen from measurements on real phone Wi-Fi where shorter delays dropped late packets. Lower it on a good network, raise it on a bad one.
Private by design
One-time QR pairing, a verified key exchange, host approval for every device, and encrypted, authenticated audio packets. Nothing leaves your network.
Follows your headphones
On Windows, switch between speakers and Bluetooth headphones and the audio follows within about a second.
Open source
Apache-2.0. Read it, build it, fix it. The engine is Rust, the app is Flutter.

Questions

Does it work over Bluetooth?

No. Devices must share a Wi-Fi network, wired LAN or phone hotspot, because Bluetooth cannot keep several devices tightly in sync. Bluetooth headphones and speakers still work as the output of any device running Roundelay.

Do I need internet or an account?

Neither. Audio goes directly between your devices on your own network, encrypted, and never through a server.

Why is there a delay?

To keep devices in sync, each one plays the audio a fixed moment after the host sends it. The default is 160 ms and you can change it. If you can hear the host's own speakers as well, mute them or lower them, because they play live and the guests play slightly later.

Can I share audio from any app?

On Windows, everything the PC plays. On Android, apps that allow audio capture, which covers most music and video apps. Some apps block capture and will stay silent.

The audio is silent on a guest. What should I check?

On Windows, open the volume mixer and make sure Roundelay is not muted. The app shows a warning with a shortcut when it detects this. Also check the guest's system volume and output device.

Is it safe?

Pairing uses a one-time QR code and a verified key exchange, the host approves every new device, and every audio packet is encrypted and authenticated. The source is public so you can check, and the security policy says how to report a problem.
