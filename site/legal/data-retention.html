Data Retention

The authors retain no user data. On your devices, each item lives only as long as shown here.

Last updated 12 November 2026

ItemWhereKept until
Audio streamIn memory on host and guestsNot kept; played and discarded

Diagnostic traceLocal, only if switched onYou delete it
Web server logsGitHubPer GitHub's own policy

Security reports

Vulnerability reports and any contact details in them are kept for as long as needed to fix the issue and publish an advisory, then deleted from our side on request.
