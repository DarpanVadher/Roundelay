Legal Basis

Almost nothing is processed by the authors. Where information is handled, this is the reason.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Pairing between your devices

When a guest joins a host, the host sees the guest's device name and local address so it can approve or deny. This happens at the user's own request (a person scans the code or pastes the link), and is needed to perform what the user asked for: sharing audio between their devices. It is neither stored nor sent to the authors.

Website visits

Serving a web page involves your IP address reaching GitHub's servers. That processing is done by GitHub for delivering and securing its service. The authors receive no visitor data and use no analytics.

Security reports

If you report a vulnerability, we use what you send to investigate and fix it, and to credit you if you ask. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the software safe, and on your choice to contact us.

Consent

We do not rely on consent for anything the app does, because the app collects no personal data. Camera and audio capture are controlled by operating-system permissions that you grant or refuse.
