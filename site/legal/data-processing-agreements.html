Data Processing Agreements

No, because the authors do not process personal data for you. Here is the reasoning and what to do if you disagree.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Why none is needed

A data processing agreement is required when one party processes personal data on behalf of another. Roundelay is software that runs on your own devices; the authors never receive or handle your data, so there is no processing on your behalf to govern.

Sub-processors

We use none. The only third party involved is GitHub, which hosts this website and the downloads under its own terms and data-protection addendum.

Organisations

If your organisation's compliance team needs a statement for its records, this page and the Privacy Policy describe the data flows. If you need more, open an issue and tell us what is missing. You may also review the source code, as it is public.
