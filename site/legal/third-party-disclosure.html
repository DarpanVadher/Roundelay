Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell or share your information. These are the outside parties that touch the project.

Last updated 12 November 2026

GitHub

Hosts the source code, the release downloads and this website (GitHub Pages). When you visit or download, GitHub sees your IP address and request details under its own privacy statement.

Google ML Kit (Android only)

The Android QR scanner uses Google ML Kit's barcode scanning. Per the project's third-party notices, it is proprietary and governed by Google's terms. Scanning is intended to run on the device; Roundelay does not send camera images to us or any other server. If you prefer, skip scanning and paste the link.

Operating systems

Windows, Android and others handle audio capture, camera and network permissions. What they collect is covered by your operating system vendor's policies, not ours.

Open-source components

The app is built from open-source libraries (see Open-Source Licences). They run inside the app and do not send data anywhere on their own.

Disclosure to authorities

We hold no user data to disclose. If we were legally required to hand over something, we would give only what we have, such as the text of a vulnerability report.
