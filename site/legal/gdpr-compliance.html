GDPR Compliance

The project is built so that it does not hold personal data. This page explains what that means and what rights you still have.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Our role

The Roundelay authors do not operate a service that receives personal data from the app. For the app, there is no controller-run processing to describe: everything happens between devices you control. For the website, GitHub hosts the pages and is responsible for its own server logs under its own privacy statement.

Data minimisation by design

  • No accounts, no email addresses, no analytics, no advertising identifiers.
  • Pairing uses a single-use token and a QR fingerprint; paired devices are not saved.
  • The only file written is the host's own identity key, on that host.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict and port your data, to object to processing, and to complain to your supervisory authority. Because the authors hold no personal data about app users, there is normally nothing to access or erase. See Data Deletion for removing the files that exist on your own devices. If you think we do hold something about you, open an issue without personal details and we will arrange a private channel.

International transfers

The app sends nothing to us. Visiting the website involves GitHub, which may process data outside your country under its own safeguards.

Organisations deploying Roundelay

If your organisation runs Roundelay for staff or customers, your organisation decides the purposes and is the controller for anything it chooses to log or retain. The software does not do that on its own.
