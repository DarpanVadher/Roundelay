Legal & privacy

Fifteen short pages on privacy, terms, data and licences.

Last updated 12 November 2026

Roundelay shares audio between your own devices on your own network, with no accounts and no servers of ours, so most of these pages are short and say plainly what we do not do. They describe how the project works today.

Plain-language summaries, not legal advice. Roundelay is an early-stage open-source project. If you rely on it for compliance, check these pages against your own situation.
